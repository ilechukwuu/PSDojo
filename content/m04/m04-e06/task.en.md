# Task

The weekly reports of the finance department lie deep inside a share. You are tired of
typing the path and create a drive for it. The dojo passes the folder as `$Root`.

1. Create a drive named `Reports` of the provider `FileSystem` whose root is `$Root`.
2. Store the items at the top of the new drive in `$ReportFiles`. Use the drive in the
   path, not `$Root`.
3. Store in `$HasMarch` whether the item `2026-03.csv` exists on the new drive.

Do not remove the drive at the end. Leave the line with `param` as it is.
