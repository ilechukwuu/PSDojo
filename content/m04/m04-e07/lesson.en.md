# Module 4 in review

This quiz closes the module. It covers what you have used in the exercises before:

- A provider presents a data store as a drive.
- The item cmdlets work on every drive.
- Registry values are properties of a key. Certificates live on `Cert:`.
- `-PathType` and `-ItemType` say what kind of item is meant.
- Scripts use full paths and do not change the location.
- `New-PSDrive` creates a drive for the session.
