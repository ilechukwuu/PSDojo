# Module 11 in review

This quiz closes the module. It covers what you have used in the exercises before:

- Stream 1 is the result. Only it flows into variables and pipes.
- `Write-Host` writes messages for people, not results.
- `Write-Warning` is stream 3, `Write-Verbose` is stream 4.
- Verbose messages are hidden unless asked for.
- `2>`, `3>` and `*>` redirect streams, `2>&1` merges into the result.
- A tool is quiet by default.
