# Task

For the review of stale accounts you check one account by hand first. So that the result
can be verified, today's date is fixed in the first line of your exercise file. Leave the
first two lines as they are.

1. Store the date 90 days before `$Today` in `$Limit`.
2. Store the time span between `$Today` and `$LastLogon` in `$Age`.
3. Store the number of whole days of that span in `$AgeDays`.
4. Store in `$IsStale` whether `$LastLogon` lies before `$Limit`.
5. Store the date 30 days after `$Today` in `$DeleteOn`. On that day the account would be
   removed.
