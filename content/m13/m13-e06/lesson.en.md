# Module 13 in review

This quiz closes the module. It covers what you have used in the exercises before:

- `%` returns the remainder, `1GB` is a number.
- `+=` and `++` change a variable based on its own value.
- Arithmetic comes before comparisons, comparisons before `-and` and `-or`.
- Parentheses remove every doubt.
- `-split`, `-join` and `..` build and take apart lists.
- `-as` converts or returns `$null`, `-in` replaces chains of `-or`.
