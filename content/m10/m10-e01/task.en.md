# Task

You prepare three views of the services on APP-01 for the handover at the end of your
shift.

1. Store a table with the columns `Name` and `Status` in `$Table`.
2. Store a list with every property of the service `Spooler` in `$List`.
3. Store a wide view of the property `Name` in three columns in `$Wide`.

Each line ends with a `Format-` cmdlet.
