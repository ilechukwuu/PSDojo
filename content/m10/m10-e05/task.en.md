# Task

The script in your exercise file builds three results about the stopped services on APP-01
and fetches the services three times for it. Restructure it.

1. Fetch, filter and sort once and store the service objects in `$Stopped`.
2. Build `$Table`, `$List` and `$Count` from `$Stopped`.

`$Table`, `$List` and `$Count` must hold exactly what they hold now. `Get-Service` may be
called only once.
