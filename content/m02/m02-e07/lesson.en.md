# Module 2 in review

This quiz closes the module. It covers what you have used in the exercises before:

- F5 runs the whole file, F8 runs the selection.
- Comments, block comments and regions make a script readable.
- The editor marks syntax errors before anything runs.
- The debugger stops at breakpoints and lets you read variables.
- One statement per line, consistent spacing, no tabs.
- Host, version and edition tell you where a script runs.

You pass with 80 percent. If you fail you can try again as often as you like. The feedback
tells you which questions were wrong, not what the right answer is.
