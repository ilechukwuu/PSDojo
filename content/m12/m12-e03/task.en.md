# Task

One row of the capacity file for FS-02 arrives as four texts. The first lines of your
exercise file hold them. Leave those lines as they are and convert.

1. Store `$UsedText` as a whole number in `$UsedGB`.
2. Store `$TotalText` as a whole number in `$TotalGB`.
3. Store the used share in percent, rounded to one decimal place, in `$UsedPercent`:
   used divided by total, times 100.
4. Store `$DateText` as a date in `$MeasuredAt`.
5. Store the date seven days after `$MeasuredAt` in `$NextCheck`.
