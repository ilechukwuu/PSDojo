# Module 5 in review

This quiz closes the module. It covers what you have used in the exercises before:

- The pipe hands objects from one command to the next.
- Objects flow one at a time. `Sort-Object` collects first.
- The order of the commands decides the result.
- A semicolon or a new line separates commands.
- The pipe goes at the end of a line.
- `Tee-Object` keeps an intermediate result.
