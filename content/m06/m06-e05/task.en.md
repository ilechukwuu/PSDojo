# Task

The monthly usage report for FS-02 needs a few calculated values. The first three lines of
your exercise file hold the raw data. Leave them as they are.

1. Store `$UsedPercentC` rounded to one decimal place in `$RoundedC`.
2. Store the larger of `$UsedPercentC` and `$UsedPercentD` in `$Highest`.
3. Store the extension of `$FileName` in `$Extension`. Use the type `System.IO.Path`.
4. Store today's date at midnight in `$Today`.

Use a static member in every line.
