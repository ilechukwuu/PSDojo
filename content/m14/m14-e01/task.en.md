# Task

You build the texts for a disk warning mail. The first lines of your exercise file hold the
data. Leave them as they are.

1. Store the text `Disk warning for FS-02` in `$Subject`. Insert the value of `$Server`.
2. Store the text `Drive D: has 75 GB free` in `$Line`. Insert the properties `Letter` and
   `FreeGB` of `$Disk`.
3. Store the text `FS-02_2026.log` in `$LogName`. Insert `$Server` and `$Year`.
4. Store the literal text `Set $Limit in config.psd1` in `$Hint`. Nothing may be replaced
   in it.
